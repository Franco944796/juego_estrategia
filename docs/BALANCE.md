## Tuning log

The tuning log is kept by hand; `pnpm sim --write` preserves this section.

### 2026-10-31 — Snowball and eliminations

Measured with 8 bots, seeds 1–5 (variants compared on seeds 1–8 first).

| Change              | Old             | New                                         |
| ------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `towns.fortPerTier` | 0.15            | 0.25                                        |
| `towns.capitalFort` | 1.25            | 1.75                                        |
| `throne.fortMult`   | 1.5             | 1                                           |
| `throne.garrison`   | 320             | 300                                         |
| `march.landSpeed`   | 45              | 35                                          |
| Exile (engine rule) | outer ring only | any free neutral town, outermost ring first |

| Metric                       | Before | After |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Eliminations per season      | 5.2    | 3.0   |
| Exiles per season            | 0.0    | 2.2   |
| Winner share of all Dominion | 0.45   | 0.37  |
| Towns per lord at mid-season | 4.8    | 8.1   |
| Land march (game min)        | 0.98   | 1.23  |
| Throne captures per season   | 2.6    | 1.6   |

Why: towns fell too easily, so the strongest bot ate its neighbours and five of eight lords were out by
the end; exile never helped because the outer ring was full by then. Stronger walls slow the snowball,
and the Throne keeps roughly its old defense (tier fortification grew, its own multiplier dropped).

### 2026-10-31 — Live preset (30-day online world)

`liveConfig` starts from the demo values: production ÷20, upgrade, respecialization and plunder costs
×5, glory thresholds ×5, wind shift every 180 min, tier-1 capitals that cannot be attacked.

Without brakes one lord ended with 0.88 of all Dominion and everyone else kept only their immune
capital (median 1 town at mid-season). Measured with 8 bots, seeds 1–3; Franco chose the strong pair:

| Change               | Old | New                                                                |
| -------------------- | --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `towns.homeIsleFort` | 1   | 3                                                                  |
| `towns.sprawl`       | off | free 6 towns, −10% per extra town (production ÷ (1 + 0.1 × extra)) |

| Variant                    | Winner share | Towns at mid-season | Throne captures |
| -------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------- | --------------- |
| No brakes                  | 0.88         | 1.0                 | 1.0             |
| Home isle ×2 only          | 0.86         | 1.0                 | 1.0             |
| Sprawl (8, 0.06) only      | 0.67         | 2.3                 | 1.7             |
| Both, medium (×2; 8, 0.06) | 0.37         | 5.2                 | 13.0            |
| Both, strong (×3; 6, 0.1)  | 0.26         | 8.5                 | 21.3            |

Bots play around the clock; people do not, so the real world will move more slowly than this.

### 2026-11-01 — Big isles, zone beacons and faster marches

Franco asked for marches ×10 and for isles of 100 towns: the season starts with one, and a new isle is
born when the newest one is full (10 lords) or fully conquered, at most 3 per day. Each lord appears on
the shore with a new capital and 3 private neutral towns; beacons go per zone of about 10 towns; no
Throne. Measured with 8 bots (demo seeds 1–12, live seeds 1–3).

| Change                   | Old                                | New                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `march` land / sky speed | 35 / 45                            | 350 / 450                                           |
| `march.minDurationMin`   | 0.5                                | 0.05                                                |

| `throne.enabled`         | always on                          | false                                               |

| Bot zone value           | +beacon only for the last town     | +3 × beacon × (owned share of the zone)²            |

the end); limiting bot targets and sources brought it down from ~150 s.

### 2026-11-01 — Equipment

Six gear slots (weapon attack, armor troop defense, helmet walls, cloak troop production, boots march
speed, ring gold), four rarities (+3/+6/+10/+15%), one item every 5 lord levels and a Legacy shop
online. Bots wear their best items. Nothing retuned: demo first level 100 at 176 min, live at 9856 min
(6.8 days). The boots make marches a little shorter, so fewer sky marches wait for a tailwind (demo
0.49, live 0.38).

### 2026-11-01 — Grouped attacks and marches by army size

Franco asked for marches that take longer with distance and with the number of troops: up to 9 troops
reach a neighbor in under a second and cross the isle (~540) in ~10 real seconds, and each extra digit
adds +500% (100 troops ×11, 1M ×31, 1T ×61), the same in real seconds in the demo and online. Groups:
several own towns send everything, arrive together and fight as one army; only attacks count for the
limit of 10 (a group once).

| Change                 | Old              | New                                     |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `march` land / sky     | 350 / 450 (both) | demo 162 / 210 game units/min, live ×20 |
| `march.minDurationMin` | 0.05 (both)      | demo 0.2, live 0.01 (0.6 real s)        |
| `march.perDigit`       | —                | 5                                       |
| March limit            | every march      | attacks only, a group counting once     |
| Sim march targets      | land/sky medians | informational only                      |

Demo: fewer captures (568 per season, was ~1 400), PvP tension up to 0.22, first level 100 at 207
min. Live: same pacing (10 158 min) but **no bot flies to the second isle**: high-level enemy towns at
home are worth far more than the level 1–3 neutrals over there, and big armies make the flight long.

### 2026-11-02 — One isle for the 3D map

Isle growth is off (`map.growth.enabled: false`) in the demo and online while the 3D map is tried
out, so there are no flights: the tailwind share now reads n/a instead of off. Pacing is unchanged
(demo first level 100 at 208 min, live at 9 955 min).

### 2026-11-02 — Immune capitals everywhere and inland spawns

Capitals cannot be attacked in the demo either, and lords now start on a random neutral town far
from the other capitals (with its three nearest neighbors as level-1 private towns) instead of on the
shore. With nobody's capital at risk, zones are easier to hold: demo lords who light a beacon went
from 0.41 to 0.63 (target 0.75), and no lord is ever eliminated. Live: first level 100 at 7 757 min.

### 2026-11-02 — Faster online world

Franco asked for ×5 gold and troop production online (the world he tests from his phone), so the
live preset now uses the demo's production per minute; costs stay ×40. First capture 18.6 min (was
45.9), first level 100 at 3 277 min, 2.3 days (was 5.4). The live target moves from ~1 week to 2–3
days (2 500–4 500 min).

### 2026-11-02 — Power, amulet and chest

Kills against much stronger lords pay ×2 experience, against somewhat weaker ones ×0.5, and lords
under 65% of your power cannot be attacked. Two gear slots: amulet (+gold per kill) and chest (gold
back from a lost town). Demo: 7 of 8 targets as before (beacons 0.49), first level 100 at 171 min.
Live: first level 100 now at 2 088 min (1.5 days, under the 2–3 day target), 5.2 towns per lord at
mid-season (target ≥ 6) and winner share 0.36 (≤ 0.35): strong lords can no longer farm the weak, so
they grow by levels and neutrals while the weak get double experience attacking them.
